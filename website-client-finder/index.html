How scores work

Opportunity score is a transparent heuristic: no website recorded (+35), public phone (+20), business name (+10), address (+10), email (+5), and industry fit (+10), capped at 100. It is not a probability of purchase. Buying intent is a separate field and remains “Not verified” until a public, dated signal is manually documented.

Only use public business contact details for appropriate, lawful business outreach. Respect local telemarketing rules and do-not-contact requests. Do not infer private personal details or scrape restricted services.
